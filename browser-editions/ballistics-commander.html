Field manual · the physics & the code

VELOCITY is speed with a direction. Your slider sets the launch speed; the angle splits it into two variables: vx (sideways speed) and vy (upward speed).

Then a GAME LOOP runs 60 times a second. Each tick it repeats three orders: move the shell by its velocity (x += vx, y += vy), let GRAVITY eat some upward speed (vy -= 9.8 × dt), and let wind nudge vx. That's the whole trick — every smooth game animation you've ever seen is just variables being updated in a fast loop, exactly like the loops you met in Stage 1.

Watch the tracer dots: they curve because vy shrinks every tick. That curve is gravity, drawn in code.
