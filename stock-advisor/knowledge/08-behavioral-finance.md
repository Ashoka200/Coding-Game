# 08 — Behavioral Finance (CFA L3): the biases this system exists to counteract

Two jobs: exploit crowd biases in the market, and — more importantly — protect the
user from their own. The system is the pre-commitment device.

## Biases the system guards the user against

- **Loss aversion / disposition effect:** selling winners early, riding losers.
  Counter: mechanical stops and trailing rules; the system never presents "wait for
  breakeven" as an option — breakeven is not a level the market knows about.
- **Averaging down** (rationalized loss aversion): banned in the trading sleeve;
  allowed in the investing book *only* as pre-planned tranches while the thesis
  checklist still passes — "cheaper" is not a thesis.
- **Recency & extrapolation:** projecting the last 2 quarters forever. Counter: base
  rates and mean-reversion priors in all growth assumptions (module 02).
- **Confirmation bias:** every 360° report *must* contain the bear case and the
  invalidation triggers, written as persuasively as the bull case.
- **Overconfidence after wins:** risk % never scales up with a streak (module 04);
  attribution reports separate skill from market beta each quarter.
- **Anchoring on purchase price:** reports never frame decisions vs cost basis;
  the only question is "would I buy this today at this price?" P&L color is shown in
  the portfolio view, hidden in the decision view.
- **Action bias:** cash is a recommendation the system is required to make when no
  setup qualifies; "no trade this week" is a first-class output, not a failure.
- **Narrative/story stocks:** any thesis that contains a story must also contain
  numbers that would falsify it, or it is flagged as unfalsifiable and rejected.

## Crowd biases the system tries to exploit

- **Post-earnings-announcement drift:** prices under-react to genuine surprises;
  fresh 52w highs on results are continuation candidates, not "too late."
- **Anchoring at round numbers / prior highs:** breakout supply dries beyond old
  anchors (the mechanism behind module 05's momentum setups).
- **Neglect:** thin-coverage small/mid-caps misprice more often — the scout weights
  under-followed names higher when quality passes (with liquidity caps from 04).
- **Panic correlation:** indiscriminate selling in crashes puts quality on sale;
  the staged-deployment rule in module 03 is the pre-committed buyer.

## Process enforcement mechanics

- **Pre-commitment:** levels and size are set before entry and logged; changing them
  post-entry requires a written reason in the journal.
- **Cooling-off:** after 3 consecutive losing trades or any −5% day, the system
  enforces a 48h no-new-trades window (tilt protection).
- **Decision journal:** every action logged with thesis, expected outcome, and
  confidence; quarterly review compares stated confidence vs realized hit rate to
  compute and report the user's personal calibration curve.
- The AI reviewer persona is instructed to be the desk's risk-manager voice — its job
  in every report is to argue *against* the trade once, honestly, before concluding.
