# 01 — Growth Corridor Analysis (the Hyderabad lesson, formalized)

## The case study: how Hyderabad land compounded, mechanically

Hyderabad's west-side land (Madhapur/Gachibowli/Kokapet corridor) went from
agricultural values to among India's highest urban land prices in ~25 years. The
pattern, decomposed into its actual causal sequence:

1. **Policy commitment (mid-1990s):** state government decides on an IT-led strategy;
   HITEC City announced (1996–98). *Land near the announcement was still priced as
   farmland — the signal was fully public.*
2. **Anchor employer arrival (1998–2005):** Microsoft, Infosys, Wipro campuses.
   Jobs are the engine — land appreciates where **incomes** concentrate, not where
   plans exist. Each 100k IT jobs pulled housing, retail, and further land demand.
3. **Connectivity infrastructure (2000s–2010s):** Outer Ring Road (ORR, ~2008–2012)
   compressed travel time and *redrew the map* — every ORR exit became a
   micro-market. Airport shift to Shamshabad (2008) created a southern corridor.
4. **Institutional deepening (2010s):** financial district, universities, hospitals,
   metro (2017). Values shifted from speculative to income-supported.
5. **Speculative overshoot & spread (2015–):** core became unaffordable → demand
   radiated outward along ORR/RRR announcements; late entrants in the core earned
   ordinary returns; the 10x moved to the next ring.

**The generalized law:** `land value growth = jobs anchor × connectivity delta ×
policy persistence × supply constraint`, and returns accrue to those who buy
*between the public announcement and the physical arrival* — typically a 3–8 year
window when information is public but conviction is scarce.

Counter-examples the model must also learn from (base rates!): announced-but-stalled
projects litter India — capital-shift plans that reversed with governments, SEZs that
never filled, "upcoming airports" delayed 15 years. Amaravati itself (announced 2015,
stalled 2019–2024, revived since) burned the buyers who ignored **policy-persistence
risk**. Roughly: anchors + funded construction = high odds; announcements alone =
lottery ticket.

## The corridor scoring template (engine spec, 0–100)

- **Jobs anchor (30):** committed private employment within 15km — signed allotments
  in industrial parks, IT park leasing, PLI factory sites. Announced-only scores ≤ half
  of under-construction; operational scores full.
- **Connectivity delta (25):** % travel-time reduction to the metro core/airport/port
  from *funded, tendered* projects (highway, ring road, rail, metro). Tender awarded
  > DPR approved > announced. New link where none existed beats widening.
- **Policy persistence (15):** central projects (NHAI/rail) > state flagship > local
  promises; survived a government change = double weight; opposition also supports = max.
- **Supply constraint (10):** geography (water bodies, reserved forest, military land)
  and regulation limiting competing supply. Unlimited flat land in every direction
  caps appreciation (a key reason some corridors 10x and others 2x).
- **Stage of cycle (20):** the entry-timing score —
  - Stage A (announcement, farmland pricing): highest return *potential*, highest
    policy risk. Size small.
  - Stage B (construction visible, guidance values rising, plotted layouts appearing):
    the sweet spot — risk collapsed, price hasn't fully. Core allocation stage.
  - Stage C (anchor operational, organized developers entering, RERA filings rising):
    solid but ordinary returns; buy only quality locations.
  - Stage D (mature/speculative — prices need the *next* announcement to justify):
    exit stage, not entry. The system flags owned parcels reaching Stage D.

## Current watch-map logic (examples of what the monitor tracks — verify live)

- **AP:** Amaravati revival (funded works vs promises), Visakhapatnam growth +
  Bhogapuram airport corridor, industrial nodes on Chennai–Visakhapatnam &
  Hyderabad–Bengaluru corridors (NICDC), port-led nodes (Ramayapatnam, Machilipatnam).
- **TG:** RRR (Regional Ring Road) alignment villages, Hyderabad pharma/industrial
  clusters, growth radiating north/east as west matures, metro expansion phases.
- **Pattern-matched elsewhere:** Jewar airport (UP), Navi Mumbai airport ring,
  Dholera (long-dated), new state-capital or HC-bench cities. Same template applies.

## Return math discipline

- Model: entry price vs guidance-value trajectory + stage-transition probability;
  target = what Stage C pricing looks like for this micro-market, discounted for
  time (6–24mo exit friction) and holding costs (fencing, taxes, caretaking, weeds
  — literal and legal).
- Benchmark every thesis vs Nifty index return over the same horizon after all costs.
  A corridor thesis must beat it with margin to justify illiquidity + title risk.
- Position sizing: land is concentrated and indivisible — treat each parcel like a
  max-conviction stock position; total land allocation set at portfolio level (IPS),
  never "whatever the plot costs."
