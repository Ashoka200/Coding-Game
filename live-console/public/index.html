Castle in the Air

Burton Malkiel's two theories, side by side. Firm foundation says a share is worth what the business earns. Castle in the air says it is worth what the next buyer will pay — Keynes' beauty contest, where you guess what the crowd will admire. This screen measures both and shows which one is currently holding the price up.
